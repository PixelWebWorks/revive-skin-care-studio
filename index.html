Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your Signature Revive appointment.

Is there any downtime or peeling after the Signature Facial? ▾
None at all! The enzyme peel and modalities are formulated to nourish and regenerate your skin rather than burn it. You will leave with a dewy, glowing finish, and can immediately resume your daily routine.
Does Dermaplaning hurt or cause hair to grow back thicker? ▾
Dermaplaning is completely painless (it feels like a gentle scrape or light tickle). The vellus hair (peach fuzz) removed is physiologically incapable of growing back thicker or darker. It returns at the exact same texture within 3–4 weeks.
Is this facial suitable for sensitive or acne-prone skin? ▾
Yes, absolutely. Because Jennifer performs a custom skin analysis at the start, treatments are calibrated to your skin tolerance. In cases of active breakouts, extractions are handled with clinical care and the O2toDerm oxygen therapy accelerates healing.
How long before an event or wedding should I schedule it? ▾
We recommend scheduling your Signature Revive Facial 24 to 72 hours prior to your big event. This timing allows the skin to fully settle into its maximum hydration and luminosity peak.
Where is the studio located and where do I park? ▾
Revive Skin Care Studio is conveniently situated at 13 Spring St, 2nd floor, Stamford CT 06901 (inside the beautiful Verdz Beauty / Lash Brow Room suite). Street parking and nearby garage parking are easily accessible in downtown Stamford.
